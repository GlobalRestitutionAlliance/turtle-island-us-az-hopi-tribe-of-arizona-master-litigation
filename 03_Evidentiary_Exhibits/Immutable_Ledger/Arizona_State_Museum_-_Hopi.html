EXHIBITS BUILDING TEMPORARILY CLOSED

Our exhibits building is currently closed but we're still on the job serving you, the university, and the state! Read about how our work and the museum's mission are unimpeded by our extended-temporary building closure.

Join Us for Exhibits, Events, Classes, and Travel beyond our walls. 

 

As of August 1, 2024, our 100-year-old North Building (with exhibits, public programs, and library) is closed to the public due to outmoded electrical, plumbing, and other infrastructure systems. University of Arizona administrators are working with ASM leadership to evaluate options and develop a plan to accommodate the needs of ASM's collections, public programs, visitors, and personnel. Planning is ongoing, and updates will be shared as more information becomes available. 
FAQ and updates are posted here and on our social media outlets. To receive monthly updates, and to be kept informed of all museum news, sign up for the ASM newsletter.

We respectfully acknowledge the University of Arizona is on the land and territories of Indigenous peoples. Today, Arizona is home to 22 federally recognized tribes, with Tucson being home to the O’odham and the Yaqui. The university strives to build sustainable relationships with sovereign Native Nations and Indigenous communities through education offerings, partnerships, and community service.
